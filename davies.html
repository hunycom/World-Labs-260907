Frequently
asked questions

01

What is the core difference between 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) and traditional mesh polygons?

Traditional 3D meshes rely on heavy triangular polygons, UV textures, and complex shader passes that quickly degrade browser frame rates. In contrast, 3D Gaussian Splatting represents scenes as continuous radiance ellipsoids. Using Spark.js and spherical harmonics, World Labs streams 500K+ splats in a lightweight 7.7MB SPZ format, delivering photorealistic volumetric radiance at 60 FPS directly in WebGL without plug-ins.

02

How does Rapier 3D achieve 100Hz deterministic physics in a web browser?

World Labs compiles the Rapier 3D physics engine directly from Rust into WebAssembly (Wasm). By decoupling physics integration into fixed 10ms steps (100Hz) and binding collision colliders directly to our 7-part geometric OBBs, we guarantee zero physics divergence, sub-millimeter restitution, and zero-drift box drops/tosses in standard web browsers.

03

Can World Labs Spark 3D integrate with ROS2 and physical warehouse robots?

Yes. Spark 3D provides a native bi-directional telemetry bridge over WebSockets and WebRTC. Joint states, LiDAR point clouds, and gripper poses from ROS/ROS2 nodes synchronize seamlessly with the browser-based digital twin, enabling real-time teleoperation, path replay, and remote multi-agent fleet dispatch.

04

What spatial sensor formats are supported for 3D digital twin ingestion?

We accept standard aerial and terrestrial inputs: photogrammetric multi-view RGB imagery, structured-light and LiDAR point clouds (LAS/LAZ/E57), and industrial CAD definitions (STEP/GLTF/OBJ). The neural reconstruction pipeline automatically registers world coordinates, generates SPZ splat radiance, and partitions spatial collision bounds.

05

How accurate is the 60-point raycasting for autonomous robotic picking?

Our dual-pass raycasting queries both Gaussian radiance density and oriented bounding boxes (OBBs) across 60 discrete spatial probe rays. This yields 100% picking repeatability and sub-pixel screen projection accuracy (<1.0px), enabling precise conveyor grasping, bin-picking, and autonomous drone docking without collision jitter.
